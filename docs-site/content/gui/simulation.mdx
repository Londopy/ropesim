## Analytical (F5)

Instant. Computes fall factor from climber height vs last piece, peak force
through the UIAA model with all modifiers (wet, temperature, rope wear,
dynamic belayer), the RK4 force curve, energy budget, and the per-component
safety table. Use it for iteration — every parameter change can re-run in
real time (enable auto-run in Preferences).

## Rapier 3D (F6)

Builds the full rigid-body world: rope as a capsule chain with your chosen
link spacing, climber, gear, belayer, and the rock face trimesh. Records
~2.5 s of simulation into a replay.

- **Link spacing** — fine (0.10 m) for accuracy, coarse (0.50 m) for speed.
- **Timestep** — 1/240 s default; 1/480 s for stiff systems.

## Reading the results panel

The banner is the headline: **SAFE** (green), **CAUTION** (amber — peak
above 8 kN or any component past 50% of MBS), **DANGER** (red — peak above
10 kN or a component past 80%). The table below shows per-piece force, MBS,
and margin with a status dot each.

The force curve annotates the peak; hover for exact values, scroll to zoom,
double-click to reset. The energy bar shows where the fall's energy went —
a healthy catch puts most of it in the rope.

## Playback

After a Rapier run: play/pause, 0.1×–1× speed, loop, and a scrubber with
the peak frame marked in red. Step to the peak and look at *where* the rope
is loaded — tension colour tells you which piece is working.

## Export

- **PDF report** — summary numbers, component table, force-curve sparkline.
- **CSV** — key-value summary plus the full force curve for your own tools.
- **Copy summary** — one-liner to the clipboard.

<Callout type="warning">
Rapier peak forces are estimated from climber momentum change and can read
±15% against the analytical model. When they disagree wildly, believe the
analytical number and check your geometry.
</Callout>
